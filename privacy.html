YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Information we may receive

At present, you can browse the catalogue without creating an account. If you contact the store, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the details of your enquiry.

2. How information is used

Information shared with us may be used to respond to questions, provide customer support, resolve order issues and communicate essential updates about a purchase. We do not ask visitors to share UPI PINs, passwords, card security codes or banking login credentials.

3. Payments and third-party services

If online checkout is introduced, payment details will be handled by the selected payment provider under its own privacy and security terms. The store should receive only the transaction information needed to verify and fulfil an order, not a customer's UPI PIN or banking password.

4. Cookies, analytics and hosting

The website may rely on essential hosting or technical services to display pages and maintain basic functionality. Any analytics, advertising or optional tracking tools should be disclosed here before they are enabled. This page must be updated to accurately identify the services actually used by the live website.

5. Data retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to answer an enquiry, support a verified order or meet applicable legal obligations. Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information, although no internet service can promise absolute security.

6. Your choices

You may choose what information to include when contacting the store. If you want to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have submitted, use the Contact section. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required by law.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the store adds payment, analytics, customer-support or other services. The live policy should always describe the actual website setup and should be reviewed before paid checkout is launched.

Questions about this page?
